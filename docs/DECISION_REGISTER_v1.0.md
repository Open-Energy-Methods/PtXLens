## ADR-001 — Two different functional units and system boundaries

**Status:** ACCEPTED · **Decision:** hydrogen comparison reports *kg H₂ delivered at common named pressure, composition, time/demand/reliability gate*; methanol comparison reports *kg saleable methanol at defined product gate*. Use separate native LCAs and discounted cash-flow denominators.

**Why:** electricity-to-H₂ output and methanol-at-gate do not provide interchangeable service; raw impacts per kg cannot be compared directly. Alternative rejected: multiply H₂ impact by stoichiometric 0.1888 kg H₂/kg MeOH and call that full methanol LCA; that omits CO₂ sourcing, synthesis, losses and plant manufacture.

**Evidence / falsification:** [ISO 14040](https://www.iso.org/standard/37456.html), [ISO 14044](https://www.iso.org/standard/38498.html); unit tests must reject H2-to-MeOH functional unit substitution.

**Reopen if:** gate requirements or delivered pressure/purity documented differently from frozen source.

## ADR-015 — Conditional 2023-wholesale-only incremental storage CAPEX/OPEX DCF frontier, not a real installed investment appraisal

**Status:** **ACCEPTED as the narrow L3-A mathematical/verification subgate** (2026-11-14). **L3 complete financial scientific acceptance, L1/L2, F2 manufacturer/site, P0 and combined hydrogen/methanol v1.0 remain NOT PASSED**. **Registration honesty:** research question of comparing historic equal-service operating savings against installed storage CAPEX was chosen before this study, but specific illustrative real discount rates, 2023-wholesale repetition factors and quantified DCF outputs were registered **during/post analysis** and are **not presented as a preregistered independent prospective prediction**.

**Question:** F3-C proved in the 2023 original PyNEXUS pressure-screened model that E+S40t had **lower net 2023 wholesale grid electricity settlement** than E20t with **identical every-hour reduced H₂ service**, but F2/L1 could source only preliminary empty Type I reference-vessel mass, not a manufacturer-approved installed bank or station price. What maximum **incremental ALL-IN initial CAPEX** is *conditionally* supportable for declared operating benefit persistence, real discount, lifetime, added annual operating and replacement costs? Where would a specified hypothetical CAPEX cross the annual OPEX break-even frontier?

**Inputs admitted:** identical 2023 original source F3-C four-case modelled hourly E/ES SHA certificate `dddd07d5c0a3d4f276e80af44e28cf3cfdbc93421107fb5d9e39843a0d1d90e2` and F2 source-2023 stationary Type I EMPTY reference-vessel BOM `1315d1181b9d1eab6d3368425c9571dbe295c3360935d2972403bf3c60e2dc47`; two independent source capacity modes only. Original source 2023 E+S 2023 net wholesale difference €1,124,332.269 **gross** or €1,103,463.729 **usable** with deterministic source HiGHS MIP objective error intervals, not measured site prices or actual cost-of-production. Their **different 8,760-hour hydrogen services** cannot be compared *across* modes as equivalent functional units, and neither fulfills 18.25mkg original industrial contract.

**Method chosen:** all entries in **constant purchasing-power real 2023 EUR**; end-year `t=1..N` cash flows and `t=0` added initial CAPEX. Future `k_t` is **an explicitly hypothetical nonnegative multiplier of the 2023 source annual wholesale differential**, not a future original-hour market forecast, new optimizer, 2023-2026 economic inflation correction or an automatically repeated physical project. `NPV=-C0+Σ(k_t S_2023−O_t−R_t)/(1+r)^t+(V_N−D_N)/(1+r)^N`. Solve `NPV=0` for **maximum conditional all-in incremental C0** or, under constant OPEX, invert for allowed annual OPEX at a specified hypothetical initial CAPEX. `C0_max` may be **negative**, proving no feasible nonnegative CAPEX for that *assumed case*, even though a positive ceiling does not prove true installation cost, financeability or economic preference. For each nonnegative savings multiplier, propagate the independently certified 2023 optimum lower/upper solver saving bound through the same annuity factors; label it a deterministic **solver error interval**, NEVER a price/cost uncertainty or population confidence interval.

**Illustrative values, not project inputs:** 5/10/15/20/25/30 year horizons, 0/3/5/8/12% real discount, source annual wholesale persistence 0.25/0.5/0.75/1/1.25/1.5, extra real EUR annual OPEX 0/250k/500k, optional €1m replacement at year 10, terminal salvage/decommission zero in displayed examples. **Real OPEX, capital, failure life, replacement/terminal values still NULL.** `k=1, N=20, real r=8%, OPEX=0,replacements=0` yields optimistic **€11,038,859.955** gross vs **€10,833,969.547** usable *upper capital thresholds*, MIP intervals [€11,038,720.192, €11,039,015.533] and [€10,833,952.119, €10,834,076.528]. With hypothetical +€250k annual OPEX and +€1m replacement end year10, ceilings **€8,121,129.615** gross and **€7,916,239.208** usable, respectively; neither is a vendor price, forecast or real project NPV.

**Standards and consistency:** source methodology follows discounted cash-flow governance in [DOE H2A](https://www.hydrogen.energy.gov/program-areas/systems-analysis/h2a-analysis/h2a-production) and [DOE H2A governing equations](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/fuels/articles/guidance-filling-out-detailed-h2a-production-case-study) only as a **mathematical reference**, **not** a complete H2A after-tax, financed, site-specific hydrogen selling-price model. The [ATAP real/nominal money consistency guidance](https://www.atap.gov.au/tools-techniques/cost-benefit-analysis/3-step-2-identify-the-benefits-and-costs) requires real discount of constant EUR_2023 flows, not adding forward inflation ad hoc. No taxes, financing, depreciation, insurance, co-product or missing cost terms automatically counted zero in a full result.

**Alternative methods rejected:** dividing 2023 wholesale cost by nominal 18.25mkg H₂ and calling it full delivered levelized cost; assuming wholesale savings recur as a validated future Dutch price forecast; multiplying the F2 empty steel vessel mass by the incompatible BAFU road-vehicle composite tank per kg H₂ capacity factor; selecting a spot hydrogen Hynetwork supplier in 2023 without a real customer location and separate actual molecules purchase contract; estimating `true project NPV` with only one gross-cost term and leaving actual vendor CAPEX/OPEX unknown; labeling numerical solver MIP interval as the full sensitivity to operational conditions, risk, prices and manufacturer mass.
